## The numbers the next session should not re-derive

1. **A native figure with no `pose.orient` cannot pick anything off the floor.**
   `prop.pickup(server, shuttle)` bakes as `front` and the very first substep is
   refused: `whole-body proposal exhausted: server at 0.000s;
   segment-angle-rate/foot_r 12.046 > 7.000`. Adding `pose.orient(server,"left")`
   (and `"right"` for the other body) makes it clean: the palm takes the shuttle
   at **1.83 s**, `diagnostics: []`. Same for a thermos probe on the same hall —
   this is native-figure floor contact, not the shuttle. The side view is also
   what fixes the drawn facing (the renderer's `flipped` comes from the
   orientation string alone, `core/src/motor/actorPose.ts:714`), so a pair that
   must face each other needs `left`/`right`, not `facing:` alone.
2. **`prop.*` calls are STATE, not timed** (`STATE_NAMESPACES` in
   `core/src/compiler/index.ts:259`): they advance no cursor and report
   `end = start`. A pickup's ~1.83 s of walk-and-bend must be paid for with
   silent beats (`…` = 0.55 s). With only 1.65 s of pre-roll the push starts
   before the grab binds, seeds its drive hand off the resting palm and is
   refused: `segment-angle-rate/hand_r 44.582 > 7.000` at 1.800 s. Four
   ellipses (2.2 s) is clean.
3. **The strike is on the push's first contact substep** — `at 2.207` with the
   push starting at 2.2. So a `camera.follow` written in the same brace group
   opens its window on the strike; no gap needs authoring.
4. **`force` alone is not enough at this staging; the impulse length sets the
   range.** Measured settles, strike point x ≈ 1020: library `1.4/0.25` → ~2380
   (off stage); `1.1/0.25` → −54.8 (off the floor support, which starts at 60);
   `1.0/0.25` → 82.7; `1.0/0.18` → 429.2; `1.0/0.16` → **509.0** (the open
   court). Both `82.7` and `429.2` rest on the **bench seat (y 640)**, which is
   the hall's second support and catches anything landing left of x 440. The
   episode writes `action.push(server, shuttle, 1.0, 0.16)`: force inside the
   pinned 1.0–1.2 band, `forceSec` chosen by measurement.
5. **Object placement has no lane.** `objects: {shuttle: center}` and `: right`
   stage at the **same** point — a bare placement word falls through to
   `objectPoint`'s fallback, `x = 0.5·1920 + index·72` where index is the
   object's position in the id-sorted list (`staging/index.ts:228`). `on(X)` is
   resolved as a declared SUPPORT surface, so `on(<actor>)` fails
   (`object "shuttle" placed on "athlete" but no support surface is declared`).
   With the two props this episode declares, the shuttle is pinned to x 1032,
   which is why the serve's reach had to be shortened (4).
6. **Actor lanes are the id-sorted order**: `receiver` < `server`, so the
   receiver takes x 0.35 and the server 0.65. Renaming is the only lane knob a
   narrow episode has.
7. **`camera.follow` at the recipe default `zoom: 1` cuts both players' heads**
   from the first floor contact on. The evaluator pins the viewport inside the
   painted stage, so a following camera's y runs 0 → (1080 − 720/z); at z 1 that
   is 0 → 360 and the drawn hair tops (world y ≈ 80) leave the frame. At 0.75
   the y range is 0 → 120 and the heads survive to the eyebrows. Below
   z ≈ 0.72 the heads fit but the pan shrinks to ~120 px and the shot stops
   reading as a follow. **Reproduced, and reported as the contract asked**: the
   contact, bounce and hold stills cut both heads above the eyebrows. This is
   not fixed here — it is E4's limit 4 (vertical framing is stage-limited), and
   it needs a camera or staging decision, not an episode edit.

## Could not land, named

1. **The rubber-band `projectile.settled` y does not sit on the painted floor
   line for this set.** It rests at **y 650.16**, which is the staged actor
   ground line (`0.602 x 1080`, the hall's `safeActorArea` y-clamp in
   `avoidActorCollisions`), while the props are staged at 691.2 and the set
   paints its floor at 691. The render does not show a hovering shuttle (both
   bodies and the shuttle share the same line and read consistently), but the
   initial shuttle position (691.2) and its rest position (650.16) are 41 px
   apart on the same floor. Named, not repaired: the fix is staging/set-side
   (`docs/`, `library/`), both outside this package.
2. **No racket swing.** `swing.grip`/`swing.release` are still undeclared, so
   the serve is the hand strike the motor can bake and `prop/racket` is a prop
   on the bench. One hand holds one prop, so the racket cannot be in the same
   hand as the shuttle. A reviewer who expects a racket strike will not see one,
   and that is a capability gap, not an authoring miss.
3. **The optional return was not attempted.** The 20-minute wall clock was spent
   on the serve (the pickup had to be made solvable first, and the framing took
   one re-render); S-A1 does not depend on the return, and EP's two-frame
   contact window makes one blind attempt unlikely to land. Not started, not
   half-started.
4. **A following camera cannot keep both the floor and the heads.** Named above
   (7); the shot keeps the pan.
5. **The shuttle is drawn large** — the asset's `stageWidth` 130 at scale 0.65
   is 130 x 104 world px against a 633 px figure, so at the bounce it covers a
   player's shin. Asset-side, out of this package's area.

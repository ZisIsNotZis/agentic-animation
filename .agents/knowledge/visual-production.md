# Visual production lessons

Internal staging is normalized logical canvas space; output pixels belong to
the renderer. Focus means emphasis, not maximum zoom: preserve moving parts,
then other actors and important objects, then composition emphasis. Actor
footprints, not anchor points, decide containment.

Equal or converging same-lane positions need deterministic repulsion keyed by
stable actor ID. If footprints cannot fit, fail loudly. Diagnose with stills at
initial state, transitions, endpoints, and dense groups at delivery dimensions.
